# Candidate Guide

## Taking an assessment
1. From **Assessments → New Assessment**, pick a mode:
   - **Quick Test** (10-20 Q) — a fast snapshot.
   - **Standard** (40-60 Q) — the default balanced assessment.
   - **Full** (100-150 Q) — comprehensive, all competencies.
   - **Custom** — pick specific competencies/categories yourself.
2. Answer each question — multiple choice, multiple select, numerical, or
   written/scenario. For written answers, explain your **reasoning**, not
   just a conclusion — the scoring rubric weights reasoning and practical
   application, not just a correct final answer.
3. Your progress bar and question count are shown throughout. You cannot
   see the correct answer, rubric, or "expert answer" while an assessment is
   in progress (outside Training Mode) — this is enforced by the server, not
   just hidden in the UI.
4. When you finish, your **competency profile**, **overall score**, and
   **recommended level** are computed automatically. Ask an Assessment
   Manager or Interviewer to generate a formal report/PDF from your
   assessment if you need one for a record.

## Training Mode (`/training`)
Pick competencies/categories and a question count, then practice — the
correct answer, full explanation, reasoning, common mistakes, and (for
P6-related questions) concrete P6 checks are shown immediately after each
answer. No score is held against you in Training Mode; it exists purely to
build understanding.

## What you will never see
Per §41, a candidate account never has access to: the answer key or scoring
rubric of a question you haven't finished, hidden follow-up-question
prompts, admin analytics, or any other candidate's data.
